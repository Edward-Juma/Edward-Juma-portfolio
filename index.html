About

I'm a Computer Science graduate with hands-on strength in IT support, computer networking, troubleshooting, and system administration, plus a working web development skillset I use to build real sites for real clients.

During my industrial attachment I worked inside the ICT department at the University of Nairobi, supporting staff and students directly — fixing hardware, resolving software issues, maintaining the LAN, and keeping day-to-day operations running without drama. That environment taught me more about production systems than any classroom could.

My long-term goal is to become a cybersecurity engineer. I'm building toward that deliberately: deepening my networking fundamentals, spending time in Linux, and working through the certification path that leads there. In the meantime, I'm dependable, quick to learn, and I enjoy the kind of problem that only shows itself once you start digging.
